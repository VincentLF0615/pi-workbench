Pi Workbench is a personal, unofficial desktop client for Pi.
It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Pi or Earendil.

The application icon is original artwork created for this project:
the pi glyph as folded ribbons — a rainbow-gradient roof band over a
violet left leg and a blue-to-mint right leg with a dark cavity
between them. Its palette is a nod to Pi's public branding, but the
composition does not reproduce any official mark or logo. The design
source lives in docs/icon-reference/ and tools/make-icons.py.

"Pi" and its official marks belong to their respective rights
holders. This project claims no ownership of, or official status
for, those marks. See docs/pi-branding.md in the source.

This installer bundles a copy of the pi coding agent

License), fetched unmodified from the npm registry. Its license
is redistributed alongside it in vendor-pi/LICENSE as the MIT
