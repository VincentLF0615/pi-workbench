Pi Workbench is a personal, unofficial desktop client for Pi.
It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Pi or Earendil.

https://pi.dev/press-kit
Original artwork: https://pi.dev/logo.svg
Retrieved: 2026-11-15

The source SVG retains its original geometry and colors. Desktop raster
exports only adjust transparent padding for small icon sizes.

claim ownership or official status. See docs/pi-branding.md in the source.
